Privacy

Your details, kept simple.

What we collect, why, and how to ask us to delete it. No small print you need a lawyer for.

What we collect

Only what you give us. When you sign up for the Sunday note, that's your email address. When you use the contact form, it's your name, email, what you're interested in, and your message.

Why we collect it

To send you the Sunday note, to reply to your message, and to let you know about circle dates if you've asked us to. We rely on your consent for the newsletter and on our legitimate interest in answering you for messages.

Who else sees it

Our forms are handled by Netlify, which hosts this site. We use a mailing service to send the Sunday note. We never sell your details or share them with anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

Newsletter emails stay on the list until you unsubscribe. Contact messages are kept for up to 12 months after our last conversation, then deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Every Sunday note has an unsubscribe link. For anything else, send us a message and we'll sort it within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.
